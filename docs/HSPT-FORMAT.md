# Real HSPT format used by the sprint

The sprint's timers, mini-sets, mock exam and answer sheet all come from this table (`web/sprint/sched.js → SECTIONS`). Checked Nov 5, 2026.

| Order | Section | Questions | Minutes | Pace (s/question) | Mini-set (¼ section) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbal Skills | 60 | 16 | 16 | 15 q in 4:00 |
| 2 | Quantitative Skills | 52 | 30 | 35 | 13 q in 7:30 |
| 3 | Reading | 62 | 25 | 24 | 16 q in 6:27 |
| 4 | Mathematics | 64 | 45 | 42 | 16 q in 11:15 |
| 5 | Language | 60 | 25 | 25 | 15 q in 6:15 |
| | **Total** | **298** | **141** | | |

Other rules the app follows: multiple choice only, no calculator, no penalty for guessing (so the results screen always tells you to fill every blank), each section timed separately, and paper bubble sheets (hence the printable 1–298 sheet).

## Sources

- STS (Scholastic Testing Service), *HSPT Interpretive information*: item counts and the skills measured in each subtest, no-calculator rule, no guessing penalty. https://www.ststesting.com/hp_int_sts.pdf
- Vogel Prep, *HSPT format*: the 60/16, 52/30, 62/25, 64/45, 60/25 breakdown, 298 questions, 2 h 21 min. https://www.vogelprep.com/hspt-prep
- AdmitTest, *HSPT Test: what is on it* (updated Oct 23, 2026): same counts and times; notes that STS does not publish per-section minutes and the times come from diocese pages (e.g. Archdiocese of Washington); sample-form skill mix. https://admittest.com/guide/hspt-test
- TestPrep-Online, *HSPT test prep*: section list and skill types. https://www.testprep-online.com/hspt-test-prep
- Test Prep Chicago, *Catholic high school admissions and the HSPT*: same counts and times, paper and pencil, no calculator. https://www.testprepchicago.com/catholic-high-school-admissions-hspt

Caveat: STS publishes item counts but not minutes; all five independent sources agree on the minutes above. A given school's form can differ slightly (some add optional tests), so check the admission letter.
